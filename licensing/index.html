Part 1: Commercial Software License & Terms of Service¶

Important Note: As our core products are built natively for Joomla 6 and the Gantry 5 Framework, our development practices respect the open-source philosophy. To maintain absolute compliance with the underlying software platforms, this document details our dual-licensing architecture for commercial and free extensions.

1. The GNU GPL / MIT Split-License Model¶

To ensure absolute compliance with the Joomla Core GNU General Public License, our software uses a split-licensing structure:

  • The PHP Component (Back-End Engine): All PHP code, database schema migrations, and native Joomla execution files are strictly licensed under the GNU General Public License (GPL) v2 or later. You are granted the right to modify, fork, and use this code across your environments in accordance with GPL terms.
  • The Asset Component (Front-End & Creative Design): All visual design assets, premium Gantry 5 CSS grid frameworks, compiled SASS/SCSS files, custom typography, images, and visual design layouts remain the intellectual property of our company. These components are protected by international copyright laws and are not covered by the GPL. They are restricted exclusively to domains with valid, active commercial subscriptions.

2. Commercial Product Terms & Limitations¶

For our premium Gantry 5 templates, enterprise eCommerce features, and custom managed hosting provisioning tools:

  • Subscription Scope: Commercial licenses grant access to software updates, critical security patches, and official technical helpdesk support for the duration of an active subscription plan.
  • Site Execution Limits: While the PHP elements of our software will not artificially cease functioning on expired subscriptions (per GPL principles), access to the Automated XML Update Streams and visual asset bundles is restricted based on the number of domain seats purchased under your plan tier.
  • Unauthorized Redistribution: The redistribution, white-labeling, or resale of our proprietary frontend assets, premium Gantry 5 custom particle templates, and visual mockups outside of authorized subscription domains is strictly prohibited.

Part 2: Free Products & Community Extensions Policy¶

We believe in actively fueling the open-source dev pipeline. To support local developer communities, hackathons, and self-hosting agencies, our non-premium software is explicitly free from commercial constraint.

Our Open-Source Community Promise¶

All free extensions, utility plugins, and foundational Gantry 5 particles released directly to the Joomla Extensions Directory (JED) or our public Forgejo/GitHub repositories are 100% licensed under the MIT License or the GNU General Public License (GPL).

Free Software Freedoms Granted:¶

  • Commercial Use Allowed: You are entirely free to download, install, and modify our free extensions on commercial client websites without paying any licensing or seat fees.
  • Open Contribution & Modification: You may fork our free repositories, implement optimization patches, bundle them inside your custom localized setups, and re-distribute them under the same open-source terms.
  • No Warranty/Liability: In alignment with standard FOSS declarations, all free and community-tier products are provided "as-is" without explicit technical support workflows, performance guarantees, or liability for downstream operational faults.
