Magic: The Gathering Turn Structure¶

1. Beginning Phase¶

  • Untap Step – Active player untaps all their tapped permanents (no priority, no players act)
  • Upkeep Step – Triggered abilities that occur "at the beginning of upkeep" go on the stack; players get priority
  • Draw Step – Active player draws a card; players get priority

2. Precombat Main Phase¶

  • Single step – play lands, cast sorcery-speed spells, activate abilities

3. Combat Phase¶

  • Beginning of Combat Step – last chance to act before attackers are declared
  • Declare Attackers Step – active player declares attackers; attacking creatures tap (unless they have vigilance)
  • Declare Blockers Step – defending player declares blockers
  • Combat Damage Step – combat damage is assigned and dealt (repeats if any attacker/blocker has first strike or double strike)
  • End of Combat Step – last chance to act before combat ends

4. Postcombat Main Phase¶

  • Single step – same as precombat main phase, play remaining spells/lands

5. Ending Phase¶

  • End Step – triggered abilities that occur "at the beginning of the end step" go on the stack
  • Cleanup Step – active player discards down to maximum hand size (usually 7), damage is removed from permanents, "until end of turn" effects end (no priority given unless a trigger happens)
