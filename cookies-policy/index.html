Cookies policy

JoomServe (Pty) Ltd. ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies on https://joomserve.org/ (the "Service"). By using the Service, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy.

1. What Are Cookies?¶

Cookies are small pieces of text sent to your web browser by a website you visit. A cookie file is stored in your web browser and allows the Service or a third party to recognise you, make your next visit easier, and enhance the Service.

Cookies can be "persistent" or "session" cookies. Persistent cookies remain on your device when you go offline, while session cookies are deleted as soon as you close your web browser.

2. How We Use Cookies¶

We use cookies for various purposes, including to: - Enable essential website functionality - Analyse and understand how the Service is used and accessed - Personalise your experience - Improve website security, including through the use of anti-bot services such as hCaptcha

3. Types of Cookies We Use¶

Essential Cookies
These cookies are essential for the operation of the Service. They enable core functionalities such as page navigation and access to secure areas of the website. The website cannot function properly without these cookies.

Analytics Cookies
We use analytics cookies to track and analyse website traffic and user behaviour. These cookies help us improve the Service and ensure it meets users’ needs.

Functional Cookies
These cookies remember your preferences and settings on our website, allowing us to personalise your experience.

Security and Anti-Spam Cookies
We use cookies from third-party services such as hCaptcha to help prevent fraudulent use and ensure that access to the Service is from real users and not automated bots. These cookies help secure your experience and prevent spam and abuse.

4. hCaptcha Cookies and Privacy¶

We use hCaptcha as a security tool to protect our Service from abuse. hCaptcha may place cookies on your device to:

  • Identify potentially harmful activity and protect our website from bots

  • Analyse the behaviour of web visitors and verify legitimate traffic

  • Enhance the security and functionality of hCaptcha’s own service

For more information on hCaptcha’s use of cookies and data, please refer to the hCaptcha Privacy Policy.

5. Your Choices Regarding Cookies¶

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most web browsers allow you to accept, refuse, or delete cookies. Please note that if you choose to block or delete cookies, some aspects of the Service may not work as intended.

To manage your preferences regarding cookies on this website, you can:

  • Adjust your browser settings to block or notify you about cookies

  • Use available options on the Service to accept or reject cookies
